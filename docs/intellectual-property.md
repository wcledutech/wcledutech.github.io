# Software copyrights and patents

The Publications page links to `/software-copyrights-patents/`. Records live in
`_data/intellectual_property.json`; original document copies are under
`files/intellectual-property/`. Titles are English translations of the registered
Chinese titles. Documents retain their original language and contents.

The portfolio includes nine software copyright registrations and two granted
patents. The eight available software certificates and both patent certificates
were visually checked against their names, identifiers and dates on 16 November
2026. Original files were copied without alteration and verified by SHA-256.

CAS is registration **2022SR1378618**, corroborated by the author's local CV
(`简历-成果更新至8.15-对外展示版.pdf`, page 2). Its available supporting document
is the Copyright Protection Center's certificate dispatch notice, application
reference **2022R11L1085062**. The notice states that the certificate was dispatched
on **27 October 2022**. This is a dispatch date, not a registration date.
At the author's request, CAS is displayed with its title, year, version and
registration number only. No notice or certificate link is published for this
record. Add a certificate link when the author supplies the actual certificate.

The design patent's registered title is **电子设备的登录图形用户界面**, translated as
**Login Graphical User Interface for Electronic Devices**. The source filename
mentions programming learning, but that wording is not the registered title.
The two patent holders are the named universities. Chengliang Wang is listed
as the second designer on the design patent and third inventor on the utility model.
